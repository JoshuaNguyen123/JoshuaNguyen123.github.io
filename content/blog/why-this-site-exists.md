This site started as a project list that looked tidy and explained almost nothing. A repository can show the code, but it rarely explains why I chose the awkward solution or how a "small fix" became architecture.

Most of my work starts fuzzy. I read until I have a theory, then build the smallest useful version. Reality usually finds the embarrassing parts faster than I do.

## Keep some of the sawdust

Project pages show the finished shape. They sand off the false starts and the decisions that only made sense after something broke, and I wanted somewhere to keep a little of that.

The activity page is one attempt. It records the days when my tools saw real work and nothing else about them. It is not a productivity score. Nobody needs a leaderboard for opening Cursor.

It also publishes very little on purpose: daily totals, with no prompts, file paths, source code, credentials, or detailed work traces. I would rather show a small number I can explain than a large one I can't.

## Why write any of this

Repositories remember what changed. I want these notes to remember why, especially when the first idea was wrong.

Expect short reports from the messier side of AI systems and developer tools. If I learn something the expensive way, I may as well amortize it.

I am writing this from Bozeman, and the site is still a work in progress.
